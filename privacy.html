Screen Duel

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Screen Duel ("the app") helps you and your friends spend less time on your phones. It is made by Jeremy Klasmeier ("we" or "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

In short: we only collect your daily screen time total, never which apps or websites you use. We don't show ads, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

Account information

  • When you sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier from Apple. Apple may also share your email address, or a private relay address that hides your real one, depending on what you choose.
  • The display name, username, profile picture (your "phone buddy" and background color) and daily goal you choose in the app.
  • Your time zone, so your days end at your own midnight.
  • Your notification settings.

Screen time

  • Your total screen time for each day, measured on your iPhone through Apple's Screen Time system in steps of a few minutes.
  • The daily goal you had on each day, so streaks are calculated fairly.
  • We do not collect which apps, websites or categories you use. Apple's Screen Time system doesn't share that with us, and the app is built so it couldn't.

Social and game activity

  • Your friends and friend requests.
  • Goals you create or join, and their members.
  • Your weekly league group, placement and the XP calculated from your screen time.

Screen Duel Plus

  • If you subscribe, Apple's signed confirmation of your purchase: which plan, when it started, when it renews or ends, and whether it was refunded. We use it to turn on Plus and to show your supporter ring. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Device information

  • A push notification token from Apple, so we can send you notifications you've allowed (for example, friend requests and league results).
  • A random upload key stored on your phone that lets it send your daily total in the background. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, and it can only update your own screen time.

We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To show your screen time, streaks, XP, goals and league standings.
  • To show anyone using the app (for example, when they search your username or tap your picture) your display name, username, profile picture, daily goal, league, total XP, number of friends, and whether you're on Plus (your supporter ring and "Supporter since"). Not your screen time.
  • To show your friends your daily totals, streak and this week's XP as well.
  • To show people in your goals your daily totals for the goal's days.
  • To show people in your weekly league your display name, username, profile picture and weekly XP (not your screen time).
  • To turn on Plus features you've paid for.
  • To send notifications you've allowed.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell, rent or share it with anyone for their own use.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts the app's database and sign-in system and stores the information above for us.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Screen Time, push notifications, and app distribution (App Store and TestFlight).

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, screen time history, friends, goals, league data, Plus records and device keys. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Plus subscription: Apple bills it, so cancel it in iPhone Settings ‚Üí your name ‚Üí Subscriptions.

Your choices

  • Change your name, profile picture, goal and notification settings at any time in the app.
  • Remove friends at any time, and leave goals while they're running.
  • Turn off Screen Time access or notifications for the app at any time in iPhone Settings.
  • Delete your account and all its data in the app under Settings ‚Üí Delete account, or by emailing us.

Children

Screen Duel is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.
